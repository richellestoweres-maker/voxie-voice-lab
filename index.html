Reading the result

Four things decide whether Phase 0 passes.

Does the note name match what you sang?
Sing a note you can name. If it says C4 and you sang C4, the detector is calibrated.
Does the trace stay flat while you hold steady?
A line that suddenly leaps a full octave and drops back is the classic pitch detection failure. That is the main thing to watch for. Low notes and breathy singing trigger it first.
Does the meter keep up with you?
Slide deliberately sharp. The needle should follow within about a quarter second.
Do quiet and low notes still register?
Try your lowest comfortable note. If it reads nothing, switch the search range to Low voice and try again.
